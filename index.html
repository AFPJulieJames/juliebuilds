Questions I get

Do you still sell, or do you just teach it?

I still sell. 25 years in sales, the last 12 building and keeping my own book of business, and I am carrying a number today. Everything here comes out of live deals, not a content calendar.

Do I need to cold call to use any of this?

No. I cold call every day, and it is where I test whether research holds up, because there is no delete key on a phone call. But the research work is identical whether you call, email, DM, or follow up on an inbound form. The channel is the last decision you make, not the first.

What do you actually use AI for in sales?

Mostly the work that happens before outreach. Understanding an unfamiliar industry, working out how a specific company makes money, and finding a reason to reach out this week instead of next quarter. I do not use it to write things that pretend to be me.

Who is this for?

People who sell for a living. Reps, sales leaders, and founders still doing their own selling. It is not a passive income newsletter and it is not an AI newsletter.
