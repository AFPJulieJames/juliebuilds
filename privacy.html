Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 July 2026. Julie Builds is a brand of Automated Income Tools LLC. In this policy, "I" and "we" mean that company.

The short version. I collect your email if you subscribe, and your name, email, and answer if you fill in a form. I use it to reply to you and to send the newsletter. I do not sell your information, and I do not share it with anyone except the services listed below that I need in order to run this.

What I collect

  • Newsletter subscribers. Your email address, and the date you subscribed.
  • Contact and enquiry forms. Your name, your email address, and whatever you write in the message field. This includes forms on TikTok LIVE.
  • If you email me. Your email address and the contents of your message.
  • Website analytics. Anonymised, aggregated information about pages visited, roughly where in the world visitors are, and what kind of device they use. This is not tied to your name.

Why I collect it

  • To send you the newsletter and the resources you asked for.
  • To reply to you personally when you get in touch about working together.
  • To understand which pages and topics are useful, so I write more of what helps.

Who else touches it

I use a small number of third-party services to run this. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Beehiiv hosts the newsletter and stores subscriber email addresses.
  • Google Analytics provides the anonymised website statistics.
  • TikTok collects and passes on enquiries submitted through TikTok LIVE forms.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Zoho Mail handles email sent to hello@juliebuilds.com.

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not share it with advertisers.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site, including the storefront listed as The Kit, are affiliate links. If you buy something through one, I earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It does not change what I recommend, and I only list things I actually use. Where a link earns me a commission, I say so.

How long I keep it

Subscriber emails are kept until you unsubscribe. Enquiries are kept while we are in contact and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you come back. If you would like your information deleted sooner, ask and I will do it.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from any newsletter using the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Ask what I hold about you, and I will tell you.
  • Ask me to delete it, and I will, unless I am legally required to keep it.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA. I will honour those requests regardless of where you are.

Children

This site and newsletter are meant for adults working in or entering sales. They are not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, I will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about any of this, or a request about your information, go to hello@juliebuilds.com.
